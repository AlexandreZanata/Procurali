# 19. Monetization

### 19.1 Access policy

Individuals can publish, manually discover, send basic offers, review received offers, and initiate eligible contact for free. Professionals also keep these basic free capabilities under ordinary limits. The revenue proposition is faster relevant discovery, saved criteria, convenience, expanded history, and eventual aggregate intelligence.

Do not sell a buyer's phone as the principal product. No plan is necessary to read a public request, submit a basic valid offer, or receive buyer-initiated contact. Paid status cannot fabricate relevance, remove moderation, or label an item trusted.

### 19.2 Proposed first paid plan

“Radar Pro — BRL 19.90 per month” is a **pricing hypothesis**, not an approved or validated price. The first launchable paid package should include one multi-category local Radar, new and renewed eligible-demand alerts, saved criteria, and a factual professional profile. Expanded metrics/history and higher offer allowances are optional later benefits, not promises hidden in the MVP.

Each plan has an effective version defining name, price, currency, interval, benefits, benefit limits, eligibility, renewal/cancellation terms, and service remedies. A user's commercial purchase references the terms agreed at purchase. Changing a plan affects future purchases or future renewal periods with appropriate notice, not the already-paid period.

For “monthly,” use a calendar-month interval from confirmed activation, ending at the same local day/time in the following month, or that month's last day if the original day is absent. Access ends at the recorded deadline. Confirmation of a renewed purchase begins the next agreed interval; late confirmation does not invent unpaid access or retroactively create alerts. This is a recommended business interval rule, independent of payment implementation.

### 19.3 Cancellation, payment failure, and downgrade

Cancellation disables future renewal and retains current paid-through benefits. Payment pending does not grant benefits. Failed renewal ends new paid access at the current deadline; no grace interval is assumed for the first commercial release. The owner can later purchase again.

On downgrade, saved Radar criteria are retained but entitlement-paused. Existing valid basic offers remain available and prior contact history remains intact. If a future paid allowance exceeds free limits, downgrade prevents further over-limit actions while allowing existing legitimate offers to complete their natural lifecycle; it does not delete them to enforce a retroactive quota.

Safety suspension overrides benefits without changing the historical paid interval. The commercial policy must explain remedies for restrictions and outages before sales start. Refund eligibility, cancellation notices, and commercial receipts require an approved business policy at that later gate; they do not block the free MVP.

### 19.4 Temporary Radar — Future

A seven-day Radar pass grants explicitly described matching access for seven consecutive 24-hour periods after confirmed activation. It does not renew automatically. Categories and region scope are agreed before purchase, and the pass expires without changing basic marketplace access.

Recommend deferring it until recurring Radar use is validated. Overlap with a subscription does not extend either interval automatically; a future purchase policy should prevent accidental duplicate purchase of the same benefit. Prices and limits can change through commercial plan versions without redefining the Request, Offer, or Contact domains.

### 19.5 Demand intelligence — Future

Aggregate demand may later support a business product: category demand, average maximum budget, regional interest, unanswered requests, active buyers, and demand evolution. It must clearly distinguish budget from market-clearing or sold price, and unresolved demand from product availability.

Use a minimum aggregate group of ten distinct buyers per published geographic/category slice as an initial privacy threshold; merge or suppress smaller groups. Ten is a conservative starting safeguard against exposing individual intent in small neighborhoods, not a guarantee of anonymity. Review privacy risks before release and prohibit buyer-level exports, phone access, or individually reconstructable drill-downs. This capability is not an MVP feature.
