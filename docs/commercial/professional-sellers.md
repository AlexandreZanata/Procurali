# 17. Professional sellers

### 17.1 Classification

Ask recurring commercial users to declare professional activity and optionally supply a business display name and type: shop, merchant, reseller, business, or recurring commercial seller. The account remains the same person-controlled account with the same privacy rules. Casual individuals retain free basic access.

Volume alone does not establish professional status. Repeated commercial language, declared business identity, systematic resale activity, and the user's own declaration may support a review. An active individual selling several possessions must not be forced to buy a plan merely because they hit an allowance.

When credible undeclared commercial use is identified, request declaration and explain the distinction. The recommended MVP response is transparent classification and ordinary limits, not mandatory payment. Failure to buy Radar Pro is not misconduct. Evasion or misleading identity can be moderated on its own merits.

### 17.2 Profile and privileges

Display a factual “Professional seller” label when declared. Do not imply identity certification, product inspection, better conduct, or subscription status. Professional classification can exist without a paid plan, and the seller can use manual discovery and basic offers free of charge.

The first paid Radar plan adds alerts and saved filters; it should retain the free offer allowance initially. Higher allowances are a later commercial option only after data supports the change. Any expanded allowance remains bounded and cannot permit automated spam, above-budget offers, or forbidden contact.

Withdrawing professional classification does not cancel an unrelated entitlement automatically; the owner receives a clear explanation of continued paid access and renewal controls. If actual use remains commercial, staff may request an updated declaration. Core ownership and reputation history remain unchanged.
