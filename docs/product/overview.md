# 1. Executive summary

Procuro Aí organizes **purchase intent**, not seller inventory. A buyer publishes a purchase request containing a desired physical item, an approximate location, an accepted condition, and a maximum budget. Sellers discover nearby requests and submit specific offers. The buyer reviews those offers and initiates a WhatsApp conversation with a chosen seller. The buyer later reports whether the need was resolved.

The principal business chain is:

**Request → Offers → Contact → Outcome.**

For example, a buyer requests a used refrigerator for up to BRL 600 in Jardim Primavera. A seller offers a used Consul 340L for BRL 520. The buyer chooses “Talk on WhatsApp,” and a contextual message helps start the conversation outside the marketplace.

The MVP hypothesis is: **people with a current purchase need will publish it, and people who have a suitable item will respond.** Validation requires local demand, relevant offers, buyer-initiated contact, and an observable outcome. Registration totals alone do not validate the hypothesis.

The marketplace does not confirm a sale, reserve an item, process the item purchase, deliver goods, or observe the WhatsApp conversation. A contact initiation is measurable; an actual conversation or sale must not be inferred from that initiation. Completion is the buyer's report that the need was resolved and may include a purchase made outside the platform.

Launch in one selected city to concentrate supply and demand. City selection is an operational launch decision, not a reason to introduce nationwide discovery. The same domain can later serve additional cities.

Individuals can publish, discover, offer, and receive contact for free. Professional revenue should come from discovery speed, relevant alerts, convenience, and aggregate intelligence. Radar Pro is the first planned commercial capability, after the core loop demonstrates useful liquidity.
