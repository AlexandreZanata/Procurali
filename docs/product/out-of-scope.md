# 26. Out of MVP scope

The following capabilities are explicitly **OUT OF MVP SCOPE**:

- Internal chat or a replacement for WhatsApp.
- Calls, audio messages, or video conversations.
- Storage of negotiation media or WhatsApp conversation history.
- Item-purchase payments, checkout, wallets, escrow, or transaction guarantees.
- Delivery, fulfillment, logistics, or tracking.
- Advanced maps, exact-distance matching, or radius discovery.
- Auctions, bidding contests, item reservations, or a required formal offer acceptance.
- Complex star ratings, open-ended public reviews, or a composite trust score.
- Social feeds, followers, or gamification.
- A services marketplace, real estate, motor vehicles, jobs, food, medication, or animal trading.
- Sophisticated AI, advanced recommendation, or automated seller offer generation.
- Radar subscription sales, temporary paid passes, elaborate commercial tiers, or buyer-level intelligence exports in the initial free release.
- Identity-document collection as a universal registration requirement.
- Seller inventory catalogs as the primary marketplace structure.

Subscription payments are a later commercial capability distinct from item-purchase payments. Neither is required for the initial free MVP; no item-purchase payment capability is implied by future Radar monetization.

This specification also leaves technology selection, implementation architecture, programming, database design, technical interfaces, visual screens, and design systems to a separate later phase. They are outside this document's mandate.
