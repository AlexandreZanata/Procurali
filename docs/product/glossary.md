# 3. Domain glossary

- **User:** One registered person controlling one account and one current verified contact number.
- **Buyer:** A user acting as the author of a purchase request.
- **Individual seller:** A user offering an item in a casual personal capacity.
- **Professional seller:** A user who declares recurring commercial activity; this is a business classification, not a separate required account.
- **Purchase request / Request:** A specific, current need for a physical item, within a budget and locality.
- **Request revision:** A recorded version of the purchase requirements. A material revision changes the substance of the need.
- **Activation cycle:** One publication period. Initial publication starts cycle one; each successful renewal starts a new cycle.
- **Offer:** A seller's specific representation that an available item matches a request revision and budget.
- **Live offer:** A sent, viewed, or contacted offer that remains eligible for buyer contact under all current restrictions.
- **Relevant offer:** A valid offer matching category, requested item, accepted condition, budget, and explicit geographic scope, excluding withdrawn, invalidated, or moderated content.
- **Contact initiation:** A valid buyer action requesting a contextual WhatsApp handoff for a live offer. It is not proof that WhatsApp opened or a conversation occurred.
- **Repeat handoff:** A subsequent valid WhatsApp opening for the same buyer, seller, request cycle, and offer; it does not count as a new unique contact.
- **Outcome:** The buyer's declaration that the request was resolved, is still unresolved, or is no longer wanted.
- **Platform-attributed completion:** A buyer-reported resolution explicitly linked to an offer with a recorded contact; attribution remains self-reported.
- **Block:** A relationship preventing new marketplace interaction between two users, without deleting prior events.
- **Report:** A user-submitted allegation about a user, request, offer, or interaction.
- **Moderation action:** An accountable restriction or restoration decision, with an actor, reason, and affected scope.
- **Professional profile:** Minimal optional business identity information, separate from subscription status.
- **Radar:** A professional seller's saved matching criteria for new local demand.
- **Radar match:** A deduplicated match between a Radar and a newly eligible request cycle.
- **Commercial entitlement:** A time-bounded right to use a plan benefit; it does not change ownership or safety permissions.
- **Liquidity:** The ability of current local demand to receive relevant offers promptly and lead to buyer contact.
- **Publication cohort:** Requests grouped by their first publication within a defined city and time interval.
- **Unavailable:** A seller's indication that the offered item can no longer be offered; it withdraws the offer.
- **Removal:** Owner-requested concealment of content or deletion of an account, subject to limited safety-history preservation.
