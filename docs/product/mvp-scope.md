# 24. Mandatory MVP

### 24.1 Included capabilities

The launch must complete the request–offer–contact–outcome loop with basic safe operation:

1. Public understanding and same-city discovery of eligible physical-product requests.
2. Minimal registration, phone-control confirmation, recovery, profile locality/phone changes, and deletion behavior.
3. One account acting as both buyer and seller; simple declared professional classification without paid dependence.
4. Request drafts, validation, publication, revisions, deterministic limits, seven-day expiry, explicit renewal, completion, cancellation, and owner removal.
5. Local filters and simple deterministic ordering, with direct eligible request links.
6. Specific within-budget seller offers, private received-offer comparison, first-view recording, editing, decline, withdrawal, and lifecycle effects.
7. Buyer-initiated contextual WhatsApp handoff with current eligibility checks, unique-contact measurement, and no internal chat.
8. Outcome prompts and manual reporting with unknown/elsewhere/platform attribution distinctions.
9. Sharing through supported WhatsApp/device actions and link copying, with intent/landing attribution limits.
10. Basic factual reputation and optional structured operational feedback, without a composite public score.
11. Participant blocks, reports, extensible prohibited-content policy, account/content restriction, appeal access, and minimum auditable staff operation.
12. Core business events, cohort-aware liquidity/contact indicators, and data-retention/privacy notices.

Operational notices are minimal business messages for offer receipt, material changes, withdrawal, closure, expiry, and moderation. In-product notice availability satisfies the MVP; external automated messaging is not a dependency.

### 24.2 Delivery gates

- **Core-loop gate:** AC-01 through AC-31 demonstrate understanding, publication, local supply, private comparison, contact, and distinguishable outcomes.
- **Growth and protection gate:** AC-32 through AC-44 and AC-49 demonstrate sharing continuity, blocks, reports, moderation, deletion, reputation, policy exclusion, and free professional participation.
- **Measurement gate:** AC-48 and AC-50 demonstrate non-inflated event and policy semantics. All mandatory workflow events have defined business causes and responsible owners.
- **Operating gate:** An accountable launch owner confirms city, category policy, provisional thresholds, support/review coverage, retention notices, and permission grants before real public use.

AC-45 through AC-47 belong to the later commercial gate. Their absence does not block a free MVP. No automated item-purchase or commercial subscription flow is required to validate the core hypothesis.

### 24.3 How to assess validation

Review weekly city/category cohorts for offer coverage, first-offer delay, request-contact coverage, the North Star, outcome response, and incident burden. A growing cohort with relevant timely offers and broad buyer contact supports the hypothesis; registrations or repeated renewals without offers do not.

The brief supplies no validated numeric launch-success threshold. Do not invent one as established fact. At launch, record the team's experiment duration, minimum cohort sample, and provisional improvement targets, then assess mature cohorts consistently. Product validation and permission to expand cities require an explicit product review; core business behavior remains implementable regardless of those experiment targets.
